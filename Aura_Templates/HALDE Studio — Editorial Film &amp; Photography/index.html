(07) FAQ

Before you ask

How long does a typical project take?

Campaign films run 4–6 weeks from kickoff to final delivery. Stills-only shoots often deliver within 2 weeks. Rush timelines are possible with an adjusted scope.

Do you work with remote or existing footage?

Yes. Roughly a third of our work is post-only: editing, grading, sound, and vertical reformats from footage you already own or capture with our remote direction kit.

What does a project cost?

Post-only edits start around €4k. Single-day productions with film and stills typically land between €12k–€25k depending on crew, locations, and deliverables. Every proposal is fixed-price.

What formats do you deliver?

Every master ships in 16:9, 1:1, 9:16, and 4:5 with clean and subtitled versions. Stills are delivered graded, in web and print resolutions, with full usage rights.

Do you offer ongoing retainers?

Yes — monthly content retainers cover a set number of shoot days and edits at a reduced day rate, with priority scheduling. Most retainer clients start with one project first.
