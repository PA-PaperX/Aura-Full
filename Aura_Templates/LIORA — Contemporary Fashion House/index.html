Our process

A slow-fashion process for garments that feel refined, personal, and easy to wear.

01 / Sketch

Start with mood

We begin with references, fabric emotion, proportion, and the feeling each piece should carry.

02 / Fabric

Choose the texture

We select tactile materials that drape beautifully and support the shape of the collection.

03 / Fitting

Refine the silhouette

We test the garment on the body, refine lines, adjust details, and keep the piece wearable.

04 / Release

Drop with intention

We release in curated capsules with campaign imagery, styling notes, and limited production.

Care notes

Before you order.

What is the LIORA style? +

Minimal, soft, and elevated. The pieces are designed for modern wardrobes, quiet occasions, travel, dinners, and everyday rituals.

How should I choose my size? +

Choose your usual size for a relaxed fit. For a sharper silhouette, size down. You can also email us for styling and sizing help.

Are the fabrics delicate? +

Some pieces use delicate natural textures. We recommend gentle wash, low heat, and hanging or laying flat to dry when possible.

Do capsule pieces restock? +

Some core pieces return, but many seasonal garments are produced in limited quantities and may not restock.
