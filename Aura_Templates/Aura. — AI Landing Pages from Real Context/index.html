( FAQs )

Your Questions, Answered

A quick guide to how Aura turns context into landing pages.

Do I need a DESIGN.md file?

No. You can start from a prompt or screenshots, and Aura can generate a DESIGN.md for you so future pages stay consistent.

Can I use screenshots as references?

Yes. Drop in screenshots of pages you admire and Aura extracts layout, tone, and typography direction as generation context.

Can Aura generate images for the page?

Yes. Aura can generate contextual image batches from the surrounding page copy, then you can regenerate the images that feel off-brand.

Can I turn images into video loops?

Yes. Any generated image can become a short hover or ambient loop, prompted directly from the section it lives in.

Can I export to Figma or HTML?

Both. Pages export as clean HTML, Figma frames, or React-ready sections — plus a shareable live preview for review.

How should I avoid generic AI design?

Feed Aura real context: a DESIGN.md, references, and clear page goals. The more specific the inputs, the more your page looks like yours.
